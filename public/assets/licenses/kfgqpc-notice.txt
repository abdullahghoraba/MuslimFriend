King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex (KFGQPC) — Madinah, Saudi Arabia
https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/en/techquran/dev/

Used in the Madinah mushaf pages of this app:

- KFGQPC Uthmanic Script HAFS font, version 18 (KFGQPC-UthmanicHafs-18.woff2), distributed free of charge by the
  Complex for displaying the Quran text.
- QPC Hafs Quran text (assets/quran/madinah-qpc.json), the Complex's Unicode Uthmanic text, word by word as published
  through the Quran.com API, laid out on the lines of the Complex's 1421 AH Madinah print.

The words are shown as published. To fill each line as the print does, the screen adds tatweel (kashida) inside
some words; this draws letters out and changes no letter or mark. Line positions were checked against the Complex's own Hafs data
(page, first and last line of every ayah).
